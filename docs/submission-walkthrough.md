## Final three-minute route

Use the [November 3 validation report](nov3-autonomous-society-validation.md) as the evidence index. This is a recording script, not a video already produced. Do not start new live inference merely to reproduce these scenes.

1. **0:00–0:25 — The society.** Show Unity Gardens and its sixteen residents. Say: “We create the world, its rules and its possibilities. The residents create their own stories.” Select a home and workplace. Keep the **Mock / backend** label visible.
2. **0:25–0:55 — Ordinary life with consequences.** Follow an actual activity and open the resident's phone. Show finite food, paid work, contacts, messages or an ended call. Say: “This local view uses deterministic offline policies. Knowing someone, holding their number, talking and committing to work are different.” Calls have no voice audio. Do not send a player message to manufacture a response.
3. **0:55–1:40 — A real Muse-mediated visit.** Clearly switch to **recorded live evidence**, [the 200-response report](nov3-sustained-muse-live-evaluation.md) and [exact decisions](7g-continuity-followup-live-decisions.json). Show Sadiq's work enquiry **53**, Tunde's independent clarification/invitation **154**, Sadiq's travel choice **170**, authoritative arrival at **487.25s**, and arrival text **189**. Say: “He travelled as invited, then told Tunde he'd arrived. No job was open, so the world did not invent one.” This is a workplace visit and follow-up, not a completed employment agreement.
4. **1:40–2:05 — Independent social choices.** Show Musa's voluntary number sharing **68/87**; Sadiq and Grace gained his number without reciprocal access or automatic friendship. Briefly show the **separate earlier** [Grace–Maryam run](milestone-7g-informed-autonomy-live-evaluation.md): introduction **71**, response **75**, invitation **80**, acceptance **84**, separate joins **87/92**. Say: “They independently agreed and both attended. Sustained conversation during that hangout was not completed in the evidence.” Do not splice these runs into one timeline.
5. **2:05–2:40 — An economy and recovery.** Show four recorded paid supply orders delivering twenty food units, completed wage work and actual purchases/consumption. Contrast Zainab's rejected encounter **54** with her fresh text decision **61** and Amina's reply **66**. Say: “An ordinary mistake becomes private feedback; the next choice is generated independently. No automatic correction or stock refill.” Keep future promises distinct from completed effects.
6. **2:40–3:00 — What is proven and what remains open.** Say: “Two hundred Muse responses ran within a bounded, audited evaluation. Some residents rested and ate; some made poor priorities. Extended social hangouts and lasting friendship remain unproven. We are building a society that experiences consequences and keeps living—not a predetermined success story.” End on the town view and project title.
